# Living animals: reference-specific fidelity

Identify the actual target before modeling: scientific/common name, breed/type where relevant, adult/juvenile, sex, body condition, coat, horn status, and pose. State chosen defaults. A generic quadruped labeled with a species name is not a valid reference profile.

## Reference dossier

Use veterinary anatomy, university extension, breed associations, museum collections, direct measurement, and well-documented footage. Prefer several complementary references over many copies of the same image. Record URL, title, publisher, access date, trait supported, and limitations. A photograph does not establish unseen geometry or a numerical proportion without a scale and suitable camera angle.

The specification's initial living trait IDs are `identity`, `anatomy`, `proportions`, `feet`, `coat`, and `gait`. Include `body-condition` and `horn-status` where relevant. References support claims; a validator can check their presence but cannot certify their relevance or truth.

Choose matched side/front/rear and three-quarter references. Separate individual variability from a breed ideal. Store measured values with units and landmark definitions; label authored estimates and engineering ranges as such. Do not invent species-wide tolerances from a few photographs.

## Clay review

Compare skull/muzzle, ear and eye placement, neck attachment, shoulder, ribcage, spine, pelvis, limb segment lengths, joint directions, feet, and tail. Review the selected sex and body condition. Look for ballooned joints, overly cylindrical necks, missing bony landmarks, and an outline that only resembles the animal after coat markings are applied.

The animal's animation skeleton may simplify biological anatomy for control. Label it a rig if displayed. Internal bones, muscles, and organs require separate aligned geometry and reference work.

## Movement review

Use recorded movement of the selected animal/type to check stance, footfall order, stride reach, head/neck contribution, and habitual posture. Do not extrapolate walk correctness into trot or gallop support. Inspect contact and deformation over full cycles and at parameter limits.

If exact dimensions or motion evidence are unavailable, record that gap and keep the output in review. Do not compensate with an unsupported accuracy claim.

## Publication gate

Approve a living showcase only after evidence review, matched clay views, material views, motion/contact review, and interaction/export checks are recorded. A software pass is necessary but not sufficient. The supplied Holstein and Thoroughbred guides are starting dossiers, not biological certification.
